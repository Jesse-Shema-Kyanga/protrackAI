Project Title:
“ProTrackAI: Intelligent Employee Productivity Management System”
Focus:
Employee Performance Optimization and Data-Driven Workforce Management
________________


Project Overview:
ProTrackAI is a smart employee productivity management system designed to help organizations like MTN Rwanda monitor, evaluate, and optimize workforce performance using intelligent analytics. The system integrates data from various departments and work processes to offer real-time insights into productivity, time management, and performance trends.
________________


Key Features:
1. Real-Time Task Monitoring & Reporting
* Track employee activities and task completion rates.
* Generate daily, weekly, and monthly performance summaries.
2. Performance Analytics Dashboard
* Visualize key productivity indicators (KPIs) per department or individual.
* Identify high performers and areas needing improvement.
3. Time & Attendance Management
* Monitor check-in/check-out times and attendance trends.
* Automate alerts for late arrivals, absences, or missed deadlines.
4. Goal Setting & Evaluation
* Allow supervisors to assign targets and monitor achievement levels.
* Integrate evaluation metrics for annual or quarterly reviews.
5. Employee Feedback & Self-Evaluation Portal
* Enable employees to assess their progress and receive real-time feedback.
* Promote transparency and encourage self-improvement.
________________


Expected Impact:
* Boosts Productivity: Streamlines work processes and accountability.
* Informs HR Decisions: Provides a data-driven foundation for promotions, training, and HR interventions.
* Enhances Transparency: Builds a culture of responsibility and continuous improvement.
* Supports Remote & On-Site Work: Tracks productivity regardless of work location.
________________


Potential Applications:
* Large Enterprises: Workforce performance management across departments.
* HR Departments: Centralized monitoring for evaluations and reviews.
* Telecommunications & Service Industries: Track productivity of call centers, IT teams, and field staff.
